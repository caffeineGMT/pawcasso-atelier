## DOs and DON'Ts

### DO:
✅ Personalize high-priority emails with specific details
✅ Include your discount code prominently
✅ Make the ask clear and easy ("2 minutes", "just upvote and comment")
✅ Send follow-up on launch day with direct PH link
✅ Thank people who support you (reply to every response)
✅ Offer free sample portraits to close contacts

### DON'T:
❌ Spam people with multiple emails before they respond
❌ Send generic mass emails to personal contacts
❌ Ask for upvotes without explaining what the product does
❌ Forget to include the ProductHunt link on launch day
❌ Sound desperate or pushy ("PLEASE UPVOTE!!!")
❌ Send emails after 9 PM PT (respect people's time)

---

## CONTINGENCY PLANS

### If open rates are low (< 30%)
**Action:** Resend to unopened with new subject line at 2 PM PT
**New Subject:** "Oops, did this get buried? ProductHunt launch today 🚀"

### If click rates are low (< 10%)
**Action:** Simplify email — remove everything except PH link and one sentence
**Example:** "We're live on ProductHunt! Upvote us here: [link]. Takes 30 seconds. Thank you! — Michael"

### If people ask "What's ProductHunt?"
**Response:** "ProductHunt is like Hacker News for new products. It's where people discover cool new tech/apps. Being featured on the homepage can drive thousands of visitors, which would be huge for us. Upvoting helps us get there. No account needed — just click and upvote. Thank you!"
