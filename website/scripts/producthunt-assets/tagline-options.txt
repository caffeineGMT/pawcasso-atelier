### Option A - Value Focus
**Tagline:** "AI pet portraits in 16 art styles for just $9"

**Target persona:** Budget-conscious pet owners

**Tagline:** "Turn your pet into a Renaissance masterpiece"
**Length:** 46 chars
**Why it works:** Aspirational, specific style reference, emotional
**Target persona:** Art lovers, gift buyers
**Weaknesses:** Only mentions one style (we have 16)

### Option C - Speed Focus
**Tagline:** "AI pet portraits delivered in 24 hours"
**Length:** 40 chars
**Why it works:** Fast turnaround USP, convenience
**Target persona:** Last-minute gift buyers, impulse purchasers
**Weaknesses:** Doesn't mention price or style variety

### Option D - Emotional Hook
**Tagline:** "Where art meets animal. Custom pet portraits, $9."
**Length:** 51 chars
**Why it works:** Brand name callback, emotional + rational appeal
**Target persona:** Pet parents who see pets as family
**Weaknesses:** Slightly longer, less punchy

### Option E - Feature Stack (RECOMMENDED)
**Tagline:** "AI pet portraits. 16 styles. $9. Delivered in 24hrs."
**Length:** 54 chars
**Why it works:** Packs max info, benefit-stacking, urgency
**Target persona:** ProductHunt browsers (feature-driven)
**Weaknesses:** Can feel cluttered, lacks emotion
