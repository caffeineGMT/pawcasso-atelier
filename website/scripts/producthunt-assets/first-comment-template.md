### Common questions to prepare for:

**Q: "How is this different from just using DALL-E/Midjourney myself?"**
A: Great question! Three key differences:
1️⃣ **Prompt engineering:** We've spent 100+ hours refining prompts for each style to get consistent, high-quality results. Our Renaissance portraits actually look like Renaissance paintings, not generic AI art.
2️⃣ **Quality control:** Every image is reviewed by a human. We regenerate bad outputs (happens ~15% of the time).
3️⃣ **Time savings:** Most people don't want to learn prompt engineering or wait through 20 generations. We deliver polished results in 24 hours.

---

**Q: "Can I get a refund if I don't like it?"**
A: Absolutely. We offer unlimited revisions within 14 days. If we can't get it right after 3 tries, full refund, no questions asked. Hasn't happened yet, but the policy stands.

---

**Q: "What animals do you support? Just dogs/cats?"**
A: Any animal! We've done dogs, cats, birds, hamsters, rabbits, even a bearded dragon. If you can photograph it, we can paint it. Most exotic so far: a customer's pet hedgehog in Baroque style. Came out amazing.

---

**Q: "Do you train AI on uploaded photos?"**
A: Nope. Your photos are used only to generate your portraits, then deleted after 30 days. We don't train models on customer data. Privacy first.

---

**Q: "How do you make money at $9?"**
A: Honest answer: Margins are thin (~$4 profit per portrait after Stripe fees, API costs, hosting). The bet is on volume. 1,000 portraits/month = $4k profit, enough to keep improving the product. Long-term, we'll add print fulfillment (higher margins) and B2B (shelters, vet clinics buying in bulk).

---

**Q: "Can I use the portrait commercially?"**
A: Yes! You get full commercial rights. Use it for merch, branding, social media, whatever. Only restriction: don't resell the raw AI service (i.e., don't compete with us directly).

---

**Q: "What's the resolution? Can I print it?"**
A: 4000×5000 pixels (20 megapixels). Print-quality up to 16×20 inches at 300 DPI. We recommend 11×14 or smaller for best results.

---

**Q: "Why not offer a free tier?"**
A: Considered it, but API costs make it unsustainable. Free tier would attract tire-kickers and drain resources. $9 is low enough to be impulse-friendly but high enough to filter for serious customers.

---
