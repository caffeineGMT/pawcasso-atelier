## Group Rules Compliance Checklist

**Before posting in any group:**
- [ ] Read pinned posts and group rules
- [ ] Check if self-promotion is allowed (most ban it)
- [ ] Look for "Promotion Friday" or designated threads
- [ ] Never post direct links in main feed unless explicitly allowed
- [ ] Use comments/DMs for sharing links when asked

**Safe posting zones:**
- "Show off your pet" threads (photos only, link in profile)
- "What's your pet's name?" engagement threads
- Help/advice threads (add value first)
- Photo challenges/contests

---

## Red Flags to Avoid

❌ **Never do this:**
- Post "Check out my website!" in main feed
- Comment with links under others' photos
- DM people unsolicited promotions
- Flood with multiple posts per day
- Use the same caption repeatedly (spam detection)

✅ **Always do this:**
- Genuinely engage with others' posts first
- Share beautiful content that stands on its own
- Answer the question: "Where did you get that?" naturally
- Build relationships over weeks, not days
- Add value before asking for anything
