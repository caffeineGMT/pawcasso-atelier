## 7. Group-Specific Adjustments

### Rescue Groups
- Emphasize the emotional connection
- Share rescue story if applicable
- Focus on transformation and love
- De-emphasize price, emphasize meaning

### Breed-Specific Groups
- Highlight breed characteristics in caption
- Use breed-specific terminology
- Engage with breed discussions (not just promotion)
- Share breed-appropriate art styles

### Senior Dog Groups
- Focus on legacy and memory
- Gentler, more emotional tone
- Emphasize tribute/memorial aspect
- Never be salesy - just share beauty

### Dog Mom/Dad Groups
- Lean into the humor and community
- Use "dog parent" language
- Share gift-giving stories
- More casual, meme-friendly tone

---

## 8. Posting Frequency Guidelines

**Per Group:**
- Large groups (500K+): 1 post every 7-10 days
- Medium groups (100K-500K): 1 post every 10-14 days
- Small groups (<100K): 1 post every 14-21 days

**Comments:**
- 2-3 genuine comments per day across all groups
- Always engage before posting your own content
- Like and react to others' posts daily

**DMs:**
- Only respond when people reach out first
- Never cold DM people
- Be helpful and friendly, not salesy

## 10. Don'ts - NEVER DO THESE

❌ Post the same caption in multiple groups (spam detection)
❌ Comment with links on others' posts (instant ban)
❌ Use copy-paste promotional language
❌ Post more than once per week in the same group
❌ Ignore comments on your posts (looks like spam)
❌ DM people who didn't ask for info
❌ Post in groups where you've never engaged
❌ Share referral/discount codes publicly (looks MLM-ish)
❌ Use all caps or excessive emojis
❌ Tag the group admins in promotional posts

---

## 11. Crisis Management

### If accused of spam:

```
I'm so sorry if it came across that way! I'm a genuine dog parent (Alfie, Border Collie)
and just excited to share what I found. I'll dial it back - thanks for letting me know! 🙏
```

### If post gets deleted:

```
(DM to admin)
Hi! I noticed my post was removed. I apologize if it violated group rules.
Can you let me know what I did wrong so I don't repeat the mistake?
I'm here to be part of the community, not to spam. Thanks!
```

### If temporarily banned:

- Wait out the ban period
- Review group rules again
- Engage for 2 weeks before posting again
- Adjust approach to be more subtle
