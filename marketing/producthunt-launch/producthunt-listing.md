## Full Description (Long - appears on product page)

**Turn your pet into a work of art.**

Pawcasso Atelier uses AI to transform any pet photo into museum-quality artwork in 24 hours. No artist needed. No expensive commissions. No waiting weeks.

**🎨 How it works:**

1. **Upload** a photo of your pet (dog, cat, hamster, bird — any animal)
2. **Choose** from 17 artistic styles (Renaissance, Baroque, Pixar 3D, Needle Felt, Cyberpunk, Ukiyo-e, and more)
3. **Receive** your high-resolution digital portrait in 24 hours or less

**⚡ Why Pawcasso?**

- **Fast:** 24-hour delivery (or instant with Bundle package)
- **Affordable:** Starting at $9 (vs $100+ for commissioned art)
- **Professional quality:** 4000x5000px print-ready files
- **17 artistic styles:** From classical Renaissance to modern Pixar 3D
- **Unlimited pets:** Dogs, cats, birds, hamsters, even goldfish
- **Custom revisions:** Deluxe/Bundle packages include refinements

**📦 Packages:**

- **Basic ($9):** 1 portrait, 24-hour delivery, high-res digital file
- **Premium ($29):** 1 portrait + 2 variations, 12-hour delivery, multiple aspect ratios
- **Deluxe ($49):** 3 unique portraits, 6-hour delivery, print-ready files, custom revisions
- **Bundle ($79):** 5 unique portraits, instant delivery, commercial license, priority support

**🎁 ProductHunt Exclusive:**

First 100 hunters get **50% off** with code `PRODUCTHUNT50` (valid 48 hours).

**🖼️ Styles available:**

Renaissance, Baroque, Impressionist, Ghibli, Pop Art, Watercolor, Art Nouveau, Ukiyo-e, Cyberpunk Neon, Pixel Art, Surrealist, Dark Fantasy, Minimalist Line Art, Pixar 3D, Needle Felt, Hyperrealism, Art Deco

**🐾 Perfect for:**

- Pet parents who want unique wall art
- Memorial tributes for beloved pets
- Gifts for pet owners (birthdays, holidays)
- Social media content creators
- Pet influencers building their brand

**🛠️ Tech Stack (for makers):**

Built with Next.js 14 (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Stripe (tiered pricing), Vercel Blob (image upload), Manus API (AI generation), n8n (workflow automation). Fully serverless, zero manual work.

**❓ FAQ:**

**Q: What if I don't like the result?**
A: Deluxe/Bundle packages include custom revisions. Basic/Premium packages can upgrade to get revisions.

**Q: Can I print it?**
A: Yes! All portraits are high-resolution (4000x5000px). Deluxe/Bundle packages are print-ready at 300 DPI.

**Q: What photo works best?**
A: Clear, well-lit photos with your pet's face visible. Avoid blurry or heavily filtered images.

**Q: How long does it really take?**
A: Basic: 24 hours. Premium: 12 hours. Deluxe: 6 hours. Bundle: Instant (AI generation takes ~2 minutes).

**Q: Can I order for multiple pets?**
A: Absolutely! Each order is for one pet. Order multiple times for different pets (or grab the Bundle for 5 portraits).

**Q: Commercial use allowed?**
A: Bundle package includes a commercial license. Other tiers are for personal use only.
