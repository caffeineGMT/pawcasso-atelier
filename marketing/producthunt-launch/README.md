## 🙋 FAQ

**Q: What if I don't hit 200 supporters?**
A: Quality > quantity. 50 highly engaged supporters > 200 lukewarm contacts. Focus on close friends, colleagues, and active community members.

**Q: Should I submit at exactly 12:01 AM PT?**
A: YES. ProductHunt resets at 12:01 AM PT. Submitting then gives you the full 24-hour cycle to accumulate upvotes.

**Q: Can I pay for upvotes?**
A: NO. Against ProductHunt Terms of Service. You'll get banned. Only organic outreach.

**Q: What if I don't hit #1?**
A: Top 5 is still great for a first launch. The real value is customer feedback, email subscribers, and learning the process.

**Q: How long should I respond to comments?**
A: All day. Set phone alerts. Respond within 15 minutes if possible. Engagement matters.

**Q: What if the demo video isn't perfect?**
A: Ship it anyway. Authenticity > polish. You can always improve for the next launch.
