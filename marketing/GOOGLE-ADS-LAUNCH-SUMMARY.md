## 🔧 Technical Implementation

### UTM Tracking Structure
All landing page CTAs include proper UTM parameters:
- **Source:** `google`
- **Medium:** `cpc`
- **Campaign:** `ai-tech`, `affordable-fast`, or `memorial-gift`
- **Content:** Varies by CTA placement (hero-cta, pricing-basic, bottom-cta)

Example:
```
/order?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=ai-tech&utm_content=hero-cta
```

## 🎯 Key Decisions Made

1. **Budget Split:** 40% to Affordable angle (highest search volume), 30% each to AI and Memorial
2. **Bidding Strategy:** Start with Maximize Clicks, switch to Target CPA after 15 conversions
3. **Landing Pages:** Dedicated pages for each angle (not generic homepage)
4. **Keywords:** Mix of exact, phrase, and broad match for discovery
5. **Ad Copy:** Emotion-driven for memorial, price-driven for affordable, tech-driven for AI
6. **Target Markets:** US, Canada, UK, Australia (English-speaking, high disposable income)
7. **Negative Keywords:** Aggressive filtering of non-buyers (free, DIY, tutorial, etc.)
