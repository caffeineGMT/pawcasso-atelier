# Meta Ads Campaign Summary

**Campaign:** Pawcasso Atelier - Pet Portrait Launch Q1 2026
**Budget:** $2,000 over 30 days
**Target CPA:** $40
**Goal:** 50 purchases ($450 revenue)

---

## Campaign at a Glance

### Structure
- **1 Campaign** (Sales objective, CBO enabled)
- **3 Ad Sets** (Lookalike audiences + Cold targeting)
- **5 Ad Variants** (2 static, 2 carousel, 1 video)

### Targeting
- **Locations:** United States, Canada, UK, Australia
- **Demographics:** 25-55 years old, all genders
- **Interests:** Pet owners, dog/cat lovers, custom gifts
- **Placements:** Facebook & Instagram (Feed, Stories, Reels)

### Creative Strategy
1. Static Hero (Cat Vermeer) - Emotional storytelling
2. Static Grid (4 styles) - Value proposition
3. Carousel (6 styles) - Style variety showcase
4. Carousel (Process) - How it works
5. Video Slideshow (10 images) - FOMO/urgency

## Next Steps After Campaign

### If Successful (CPA ≤ $50)
1. Scale budget to $5,000/month
2. Create Purchaser Lookalike Audience
3. Test price increase to $15-19
4. Add upsells (framed prints, bundles)
5. Expand to Pinterest, TikTok, Google Ads

### If Marginal (CPA $50-70)
1. Optimize landing page (improve conversion rate)
2. Refresh creative with UGC content
3. Tighten targeting (US only, 30-50 age)
4. Test lower price point ($6-7 sale)
5. Build retargeting funnel

### If Unsuccessful (CPA > $70)
1. Pause campaign, analyze data
2. Interview customers (qualitative feedback)
3. Redesign landing page
4. Test organic social first (validate demand)
5. Consider different product/pricing
