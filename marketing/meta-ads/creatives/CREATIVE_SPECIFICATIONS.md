# Meta Ads Creative Specifications

## Image Specifications

### Static Ads (Facebook & Instagram Feed)
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Aspect Ratio:** 1:1 (square)
- **Recommended Size:** 1080 x 1080 pixels
- **Maximum File Size:** 30 MB
- **Text:** < 20% of image area (use text overlay tool to check)

### Static Ads (Instagram Stories)
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (vertical)
- **Recommended Size:** 1080 x 1920 pixels
- **Maximum File Size:** 30 MB

### Carousel Ads
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Aspect Ratio:** 1:1 (square)
- **Recommended Size:** 1080 x 1080 pixels per card
- **Number of Cards:** 2-10 (we're using 5-6)
- **Maximum File Size:** 30 MB per image
- **Text on Image:** < 20% per card

---

## Video Specifications

### Video Ads (Feed)
- **Format:** MP4 or MOV
- **Aspect Ratio:** 1:1 (square) or 4:5 (vertical)
- **Resolution:** 1080 x 1080 (1:1) or 1080 x 1350 (4:5)
- **Duration:** 1 second - 241 minutes (recommended: 15 seconds)
- **Maximum File Size:** 4 GB
- **Frame Rate:** 30 fps minimum

### Video Ads (Stories/Reels)
- **Format:** MP4 or MOV
- **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (vertical)
- **Resolution:** 1080 x 1920 pixels
- **Duration:** 1-60 seconds (recommended: 15 seconds)
- **Maximum File Size:** 4 GB
- **Frame Rate:** 30 fps minimum
- **Audio:** Required for best performance

## Design Guidelines

### Branding
- **Logo:** Small Pawcasso watermark (5-10% opacity)
- **Color Palette:** Warm, artistic tones (avoid harsh neon)
- **Font:** Clean, modern sans-serif for overlays (Helvetica, Montserrat)
- **Tone:** Playful, premium, accessible

### Do's
✅ Showcase multiple pet breeds (dogs, cats)
✅ Highlight variety of artistic styles
✅ Use high-quality, vibrant images
✅ Include clear pricing ($9)
✅ Show transformation/value proposition
✅ Pet-friendly, family-safe content

### Don'ts
❌ No "before/after" medical-style claims
❌ Keep text on images under 20% area
❌ No stock photos of people (use pet focus)
❌ Avoid cluttered designs
❌ No misleading "free" offers
❌ No violent/controversial imagery

## Quick Production with Free Tools

### Image Editing
- **Canva** (free tier): Grid layouts, text overlays, cropping

- **Photopea** (free web): Photoshop alternative

### Video Creation
- **Canva Video** (free): Slideshow maker with transitions
- **Kapwing** (free): Video editor with text overlays
- **CapCut** (free): Mobile video editor

### Stock Resources
- **Unsplash**: Free stock photos (phone upload, happy customer)
- **Pexels**: Free stock videos/photos
- **Pixabay**: Free music (upbeat, pet-friendly)
