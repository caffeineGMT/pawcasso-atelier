## Red Flags & Troubleshooting

### If CTR <2%:
- ❌ Weak ad copy → Rewrite headlines with stronger hooks
- ❌ Poor keyword relevance → Narrow keyword match types
- ❌ Low Quality Score → Improve landing page speed

### If CPA >$50:
- ❌ Wrong audience → Add negative keywords
- ❌ Landing page friction → Simplify checkout flow
- ❌ Bidding too high → Switch to manual CPC temporarily

### If ROAS <200%:
- ❌ Low average order value → Promote higher-tier packages
- ❌ High refund rate → Check order fulfillment quality
- ❌ Wrong traffic → Review search terms, add negatives
