## 4. COMPENSATION

**4.1 Commission Structure**

Partner will earn the following commission on all sales attributed to Partner:

**☐ Standard Commission:** 25% of gross sale price
**☐ Premium Commission:** 30% of gross sale price (for exclusive partnerships)
**☐ Custom Rate:** [SPECIFY]%

**Example Calculation:**
- Sale price: $9.00
- Partner commission (25%): $2.25
- Partner commission (30%): $2.70

**4.2 Attribution**
Sales are attributed to Partner when customers:
- Use Partner's unique discount code at checkout, OR
- Click Partner's tracking link and complete purchase within 30 days, OR
- Mention Partner's name in order form (for vet/photographer partnerships)

**4.3 Commission Exclusions**
Commissions are NOT paid on:
- Refunded or cancelled orders
- Orders marked as fraudulent
- Internal test orders
- Orders using other promotional codes (first code used gets attribution)
