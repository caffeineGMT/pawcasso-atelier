## 🚨 Troubleshooting

### "I'm stuck on personalizing emails"
→ Spend 5 minutes per partner max. Quick wins:
- Mention their Instagram recent post
- Reference a product you love
- Compliment their brand aesthetic
- Note something unique about their business

### "No one is responding"
→ Normal for Week 1. Most responses come in Week 2-3 after follow-ups. Don't panic yet.

### "Someone wants custom terms"
→ Be flexible! Start with their ask, negotiate if needed. First partnership is about learning.

### "I don't have time for all this"
→ Cut corners strategically:
- Skip Canva templates (not essential)
- Use Google Doc instead of Calendly (just offer times)
- Send emails in batches over 2 weeks instead of 1

### "Someone said yes, now what?!"
→ 🎉 CONGRATS! Send them:
1. Thank you email
2. Partnership agreement
3. Their discount code
4. Calendly link for onboarding call
5. Sample portraits
