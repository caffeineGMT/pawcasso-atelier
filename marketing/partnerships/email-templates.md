## Response Handling Templates

### When they ask for more information:

```
Thanks for your interest! I've attached our partnership one-pager with all the details.

The TL;DR:
- Revenue model: [SPECIFIC TO THEIR CATEGORY]
- Setup time: Less than 1 hour (we provide all materials)
- Expected volume: [SPECIFIC TO THEIR CATEGORY]
- Support: Dedicated partnership manager (me!)

Want to hop on a quick call to walk through it? Here's my calendar: [CALENDLY LINK]
```

### When they're interested but hesitant:

```
Totally understand the hesitation! Here's what I can offer to make this risk-free:

1. **Trial period:** Let's do a 30-day pilot. If it doesn't drive value, no hard feelings.
2. **Free samples:** I'll send you [3-5] sample portraits so you can see the quality firsthand.
3. **Case study:** I can share results from [SIMILAR PARTNER] to show what's working.

What would make you feel confident moving forward?
```

### When they ask about exclusivity:

```
Great question! We don't require exclusivity — you're free to work with other portrait services.

That said, we do offer enhanced terms for exclusive partners:
- 30% commission instead of 25%
- Priority support
- Co-branded marketing campaigns
- Featured placement on our website

Would exclusivity be something you'd consider?
```

## Tracking Metrics

Track these metrics for each outreach campaign:

- **Open rate target:** 35-45%
- **Response rate target:** 10-15%
- **Conversion to call target:** 5-8%
- **Partnership sign-up rate:** 3-5% of total outreach

**A/B Test Variables:**
- Subject lines
- Email length (short vs. detailed)
- CTA (calendar link vs. reply to email)
- Send time (Tuesday 10am vs. Thursday 2pm)

## Legal/Compliance Notes

- Include unsubscribe option in all emails
- Only send to business emails (not personal)
- Follow CAN-SPAM Act guidelines
- Don't purchase email lists — only outreach to publicly listed contacts
- Keep all partnership agreements documented
