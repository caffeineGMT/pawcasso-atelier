## 📞 Call/Meeting Script

### Opening (1 minute):
"Hi [Name], thanks for taking the time! I'm excited to explore how Pawcasso and [Partner Name] could work together. Have you had a chance to look at the one-pager I sent?"

### Discovery (3 minutes):
- "Tell me about your current customer base — what do they love most about [Partner Name]?"
- "Have you done product partnerships or affiliate programs before?"
- "What would make a partnership valuable for you? Revenue? Customer delight? Both?"

### Pitch (5 minutes):
- Walk through partnership one-pager
- Show sample portraits relevant to their audience
- Explain commission structure and expected volume
- Share early results or testimonials

### Objection Handling (3 minutes):

**"We've never done something like this before."**
→ "That's actually perfect — we can tailor this to exactly what works for you. Let's start with a 30-day pilot."

**"Our audience might not be interested."**
→ "I hear you. What if we create 3-5 sample portraits using [your photos/products/patients] and test it with your audience first?"

**"We don't have bandwidth to manage this."**
→ "That's the beauty of this partnership — we handle 100% of the fulfillment. You just share the link/code, and we do the rest."

**"Commission seems low."**
→ "I'm open to discussion! What commission structure would make this a no-brainer for you?"

### Close (2 minutes):
"Based on what we discussed, does this feel like a good fit for [Partner Name]?"

**If yes:**
"Awesome! I'll send over the partnership agreement today. Can we target going live by [DATE]?"

**If hesitant:**
"No pressure! What if we do a 30-day trial? If it doesn't work, we part as friends."

### Next Steps:
- [ ] Send partnership agreement within 24 hours
- [ ] Set up their unique discount code
- [ ] Provide co-branded marketing materials
- [ ] Schedule kickoff/onboarding call

## 🚨 Common Pitfalls & How to Avoid Them

### Pitfall 1: Spray-and-Pray Outreach
**Solution:** Personalize every email. Mention specific details about their brand/work. Takes 5 extra minutes but 3x response rate.

### Pitfall 2: Slow Response Time
**Solution:** Set up email alerts. Respond to partner inquiries within 4 hours during business hours.

### Pitfall 3: Unclear Commission Tracking
**Solution:** Use affiliate software OR maintain a detailed spreadsheet. Don't let this be a black box.

### Pitfall 4: Overpromising Volume
**Solution:** Be conservative in projections. Under-promise, over-deliver.

### Pitfall 5: No Follow-Up System
**Solution:** Use the outreach tracker religiously. Set calendar reminders for follow-ups.

### Pitfall 6: Ignoring Active Partners
**Solution:** Monthly check-ins, performance reports, and new marketing materials keep partners engaged.
