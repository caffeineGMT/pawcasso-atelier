# Corporate Gifting LinkedIn Outreach Campaign

## Overview

**Goal:** Acquire 5 corporate clients by Month 3, generating $5K+ in bulk orders through LinkedIn B2B outreach.

**Target:** 100 HR managers, People Ops leads, and Employee Engagement managers at pet-friendly companies (50-500 employees).

**Value Proposition:** Unique employee recognition and team-building gifts through AI-generated custom pet portraits. Perfect for remote teams, pet-friendly culture celebration, and memorable employee appreciation.

## Campaign Strategy

### Phase 1: Prospect Discovery (Week 1)
- Use LinkedIn Sales Navigator to identify 100 qualified prospects
- Filter for companies with pet-friendly culture signals
- Prioritize companies with visible pet policies or office dog photos

### Phase 2: Connection Outreach (Week 1-2)
- Send personalized connection requests (50 per week to avoid LinkedIn limits)
- Mention specific pet-friendly aspects of their company
- Keep messages under 300 characters for mobile optimization

### Phase 3: Follow-Up Engagement (Week 2-3)
- Wait 24-48 hours after connection acceptance
- Send follow-up message offering free pilot program (5 employee portraits)
- Share bulk pricing one-pager

### Phase 4: Pilot Execution (Week 3-4)
- Deliver exceptional pilot experience
- Collect testimonials and case studies
- Upsell to full team packages

## Target Personas

### Primary: HR Managers
- **Pain Point:** Need unique, memorable employee recognition gifts
- **Motivation:** Stand out from generic corporate swag
- **Budget Authority:** Typically $500-$5,000 per initiative

### Secondary: People Ops Leads
- **Pain Point:** Building remote team culture and connection
- **Motivation:** Create shareable moments that celebrate employees' personalities
- **Budget Authority:** $1,000-$10,000 for quarterly initiatives

### Tertiary: Employee Engagement Managers
- **Pain Point:** Low engagement in traditional recognition programs
- **Motivation:** Instagram-worthy gifts that employees actually want
- **Budget Authority:** $2,000-$15,000 annual budget

## Company Signals to Look For

### Pet-Friendly Culture Indicators
- "Dog-friendly office" in company description
- Office dog photos in company page gallery
- Pet benefits mentioned in job postings
- Employees mention pets in LinkedIn bios
- Company posts featuring team members' pets

### Ideal Company Profiles
- **Size:** 50-500 employees (sweet spot: 100-200)
- **Industry:** Tech, Marketing, Creative Agencies, SaaS
- **Culture:** Remote-first or hybrid with strong culture focus
- **Budget:** $50K+ annual employee engagement spend
- **Location:** Major US tech hubs (SF, Austin, NYC, Seattle, Denver)

## Pricing Strategy

### Bulk Pricing Tiers
- **Starter (20-49 portraits):** $25/portrait = $500-$1,225
- **Growth (50-99 portraits):** $20/portrait = $1,000-$1,980
- **Enterprise (100+ portraits):** $15/portrait = $1,500+

### Pilot Offer
- **Free pilot:** 5 employee portraits ($0 cost)
- **Conditions:** Must provide employee pet photos within 48 hours
- **Timeline:** Deliver within 72 hours of photo submission
- **Upsell:** 20% discount on full team package if ordered within 7 days
