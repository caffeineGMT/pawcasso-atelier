# LinkedIn Sales Navigator Search Guide

## Setup (First-Time Only)

### 1. Subscribe to LinkedIn Sales Navigator
- **Plan:** Core ($99/month) - sufficient for 100 prospects
- **URL:** linkedin.com/sales/
- **Alternative:** Use regular LinkedIn Premium ($39/month) with advanced search

### 2. Create Lead Lists
- Navigate to "Leads" → "All Filters"
- Save searches as "Corporate Gifting - [Criteria]"
- Goal: 5-10 saved searches that together yield 100+ prospects

## Search Strategy

### Primary Search: HR Managers at Pet-Friendly Tech Companies

**Filters:**
- **Job Title:** HR Manager, People Operations Manager, Head of People, VP People, Director of HR
- **Company Size:** 51-200 employees, 201-500 employees
- **Industry:** Computer Software, Internet, Marketing and Advertising, Information Technology
- **Geography:** United States
- **Keywords:** "pet-friendly" OR "dog-friendly" OR "office dogs" OR "bring your pet"

**Advanced Boolean Search:**
```
(HR Manager OR People Operations OR Head of People OR VP People OR Director HR) AND (pet-friendly OR dog-friendly OR office dog OR bring your pet)
```

**Expected Results:** 20-30 prospects

## Advanced Techniques

**Step 1:** Identify Pet-Friendly Companies
- Search Google: "dog-friendly office [city]"
- Check Built In [City] lists: "Best Places to Work for Pet Owners"
- Browse Instagram hashtags: #dogsoftech #officedog #petfriendlyworkplace

**Step 2:** Find Company on LinkedIn
- Go to company page
- Check "People" tab
- Filter by "Human Resources" function

**Step 3:** Identify Decision Maker
- Look for: VP People, Head of HR, People Ops Manager
- Check their activity/posts for pet content (extra validation)

**Examples of Known Pet-Friendly Companies:**

## Qualifying Criteria Checklist

Before adding a prospect to your outreach list, verify:

### Must-Have (Disqualify if No)
- [ ] Job title indicates HR/People Ops/Employee Engagement authority
- [ ] Company size: 50-500 employees
- [ ] Profile is active (posted/engaged in last 90 days)
- [ ] Based in United States (easier payment/logistics)

### Nice-to-Have (Prioritize if Yes)
- [ ] Pet-friendly signals visible (company page, posts, bio)
- [ ] Company in tech/creative industry (more likely to value unique gifts)
- [ ] Located in pet-friendly city (SF, Austin, Seattle, Portland, Denver)
- [ ] Company recently raised funding (budget availability)
- [ ] Fast-growing (20%+ headcount increase)
- [ ] Remote-first or hybrid (need creative culture solutions)

### Disqualifiers (Skip These)
- [ ] Company <50 or >500 employees
- [ ] Government or highly regulated industry (long procurement)
- [ ] International company (payment/logistics complexity)
- [ ] No activity on LinkedIn in 6+ months (inactive profile)
- [ ] Senior VP or C-suite (too high, unlikely to respond)
- [ ] Entry-level coordinator (no budget authority)

## Search Iteration

After your first week of outreach, analyze results:

**High Response Rate?**
- Double down on that search criteria
- Find more prospects matching that profile

**Low Response Rate?**
- Adjust filters (smaller companies? Different titles?)
- Change personalization approach
- Test new messaging

**Typical Benchmarks:**
- **Connection Acceptance:** 40-60%
- **Follow-Up Response:** 20-30%
- **Pilot Conversion:** 10-15% of responders
- **Paid Conversion:** 50% of pilots

If you're below these, tweak your targeting or messaging.

## Time-Saving Hacks

### Use LinkedIn Mobile App
- Faster to send connection requests on mobile
- Do it during downtime (commute, lunch)
- Limit: 10-15 per session to avoid spam flags

### Set Up Alerts
- Sales Navigator: "New leads matching saved search"
- Get daily/weekly email with fresh prospects
- Auto-refill your pipeline as you exhaust initial 100

### Batch Your Work
- **Monday:** Research 20 prospects
- **Tuesday:** Send 20 connection requests
- **Wednesday:** Follow up with accepted connections
- **Thursday:** Send another 20 requests
- **Friday:** Follow-up round 2, schedule pilots

### Use Templates (But Personalize)
- Copy template into note-taking app
- Fill in [brackets] with personalization
- Never send fully generic message (LinkedIn can flag)
