## Best Practices

### Timing
- **Send connection requests:** Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10am or 4-6pm PT
- **Follow-up messages:** 24-48 hours after connection acceptance
- **Voice messages:** Friday afternoons (lower pressure, more personal)

### Personalization
- Always mention specific company detail (pet policy, recent post, office dog photo)
- Reference their industry or similar companies you've worked with
- Use their first name 1-2 times (not every sentence)

### Frequency
- **Max 50 connection requests/week** (LinkedIn's safety threshold)
- **Max 10 follow-up messages/day** (avoid spam detection)
- **Wait 4-5 days before second follow-up** if no response

### Formatting
- Use line breaks generously (easier to read on mobile)
- Bold key points (→, ✓, bullets)
- Keep paragraphs to 1-2 sentences max
- End with clear CTA (question or action item)
