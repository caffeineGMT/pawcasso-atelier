## Board Pinning Strategy

### Week 1-2: Foundation
Add **20-30 pins per board** before launch:
- 10 Product Pins (your gallery images)
- 10 Curated content (repinned from other creators)
- 5-10 Educational/inspirational content

### Week 3-4: Growth
Add **10 new pins per board per week**:
- 5 Product Pins (variations, new angles)
- 3 Curated content
- 2 Seasonal/trending content

### Ongoing: Optimization
- Re-pin top performers to multiple boards
- Archive low-performing pins (<10 impressions/week)
- Refresh board covers monthly with best content

## Pro Tips

1. **Board Order Matters:** Pinterest shows first 5 boards prominently. Order by priority:
   - AI Pet Portraits (main product board)
   - Dog Portrait Ideas (largest audience)
   - Cat Portrait Art (second largest)
   - Pet Memorial Gifts (high-value niche)
   - Unique Pet Gifts Under $20 (seasonal spikes)

2. **Keyword Stuffing:** Front-load important keywords in descriptions. Pinterest search weighs first 50 characters heavily.

3. **Freshness Factor:** Boards with recent activity rank higher. Pin something new to each board weekly.

4. **Board Covers:** Update monthly. Eye-catching covers → higher CTR → more followers.

5. **Secret Boards:** Use for testing new content before going public. Analyze performance, then move to public boards.
