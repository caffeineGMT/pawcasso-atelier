## Content Upload Workflow

### Pre-Upload Checklist

- [ ] Video file: 1080p MP4, H.264 codec
- [ ] Thumbnail: 1280x720px JPG/PNG, <2MB
- [ ] Title: Front-loaded keywords, <60 characters

## Compliance & Policies

### Content Guidelines

✅ **Allowed:**
- Educational pet photography tutorials
- Before/after AI transformations
- Unboxing customer orders (with permission)
- Art style comparisons and reviews

❌ **Prohibited:**
- Copyrighted music (use YouTube Audio Library or Epidemic Sound)
- Misleading thumbnails (must match video content)
- Spam tags (don't repeat tags excessively)
- Encouraging off-platform transactions in comments

### Copyright-Safe Music Sources

- [ ] **YouTube Audio Library** (free, built-in)
- [ ] **Epidemic Sound** (subscription, unlimited use)
- [ ] **Artlist** (subscription, alternative to Epidemic)
- [ ] **Uppbeat** (free tier available)

### Fair Use Notice (If Using Stock Footage)
