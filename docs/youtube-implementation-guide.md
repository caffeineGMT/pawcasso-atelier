### Friday (4-5 hours)
**Editing Day — Tutorial:**
- [ ] Import A-roll, B-roll, screen recordings

### Saturday (3 hours)

## SEO Optimization Checklist

### Every Short:
- [ ] **Title:** Front-load keywords, <60 characters, emotion word
- [ ] **Description:** First 2 lines = hook + link (visible without "Show More")
- [ ] **Hashtags:** 5-8 relevant (#aipetportrait, #petart, #dogportrait)
- [ ] **Pinned Comment:** Order link + engagement hook

### Every Tutorial:
- [ ] **Title:** Main keyword in first 5 words, include number/bracket
- [ ] **Description:** Timestamps, resource links, CTA, hashtags
- [ ] **Tags:** 10-15 tags (mix of broad + long-tail keywords)
- [ ] **Chapters:** Add timestamps in description (YouTube auto-generates chapters)
- [ ] **Thumbnail:** Before/after or bold text (3-5 words max)
- [ ] **End Screen:** 4 elements (subscribe, video, playlist, order link)

### Keyword Research (Before Filming):
- [ ] Use TubeBuddy or VidIQ to check keyword search volume
- [ ] Target keywords with:
  - High search volume (1K-10K/month)
  - Low competition (score <30)
  - High buyer intent ("how to", "best", "vs", "review")

## Monetization Roadmap

### Milestone 1: YouTube Partner Program Eligibility

**Requirements:**
- 1,000 subscribers
- 4,000 watch hours in past 12 months (OR 10M Shorts views in 90 days)

**Strategy to Hit 4K Watch Hours:**
- 24 tutorials × 7 min avg × 1,000 views each = 2,800 hours (70% of goal)
- Supplement with Shorts watch time (counts toward 10M views path)
- Prioritize longer tutorials (8-10 min) in months 4-6

**Apply:**
- YouTube Studio → Monetization → Apply
- Complete AdSense setup
- Accept YPP terms
- Wait for review (typically 1 month)

### Milestone 2: First Ad Revenue

**Timeline:** Month 7-8 (post-approval)

**Revenue Estimate:**
- 50K views/month × $5 RPM (conservative) = $250/month
- Best case: 50K views × $12 RPM = $600/month

**Diversify Revenue:**
- Ad revenue: $250-600/month (passive)
- Direct orders from videos: $1,000-2,000/month (50-100 orders at $9 each)
- Affiliate/sponsor deals: $100-500/month (if channel grows)

**Total Month 7+ Revenue:** $1,350-3,100/month

## Risk Mitigation

### Common Challenges & Solutions

**Challenge 1: Low Initial Views**
- **Cause:** Algorithm doesn't know your audience yet
- **Solution:** Share on external platforms (IG, TikTok, email list). Ask friends to watch full videos (signals YouTube).

**Challenge 2: Slow Subscriber Growth**
- **Cause:** Weak CTAs or low video quality
- **Solution:** Add subscribe button watermark, improve end screens, run giveaway ("Subscribe to win free portrait").

**Challenge 3: Video Ideas Dry Up**
- **Cause:** Lack of planning
- **Solution:** Use `content-engine/youtube-tutorials-scripts.json` additional_tutorial_ideas (20+ ideas). Monitor competitor channels for inspiration.

**Challenge 4: Editing Takes Too Long**
- **Cause:** Inefficient workflow
- **Solution:** Batch film + edit. Use templates (Canva for thumbnails, CapCut for Shorts). Hire editor on Upwork ($20-30/hour) if budget allows.

**Challenge 5: Copyright Strikes (Music)**
- **Cause:** Using copyrighted music
- **Solution:** ONLY use YouTube Audio Library, Epidemic Sound, or Artlist. Never rip music from Spotify/Apple Music.
