### 4. 6-Month Content Calendar
**File:** `content-engine/youtube-content-calendar.json`

**Upload Schedule:**
- **Shorts:** 3x/week (Monday, Wednesday, Friday at 7 AM PT)
- **Tutorials:** 1x/week (Sunday at 10 AM PT)
- **Total:** 72 Shorts + 24 Tutorials = 96 videos over 6 months

**Monthly Themes:**
- **Month 1 (March):** Foundation & Introduction — 100 subs, 5K views
- **Month 2 (April):** Variety & Education — 300 subs, 15K views
- **Month 3 (May):** Viral Push & Trending Audio — 500 subs, 25K views
- **Month 4 (June):** Community Building & UGC — 700 subs, 35K views
- **Month 5 (July):** Optimization & Scale — 850 subs, 42K views
- **Month 6 (Aug-Sep):** Monetization Sprint — 1,000+ subs, 50K+ views

**Content Mix Strategy:**
- Dogs: 50%, Cats: 35%, Other pets: 15%
- All 17 art styles featured at least once per month
- Hero styles (Renaissance, Pixar 3D, Watercolor) rotated frequently

**Weekly Workflow (18-20 hours total):**
- Monday: Analytics review, content planning (1.5 hrs)
- Tuesday: Batch film 3 Shorts + B-roll (3 hrs)
- Wednesday: Edit Shorts, create thumbnails (4 hrs)
- Thursday: Upload Shorts, film tutorial A-roll (2.5 hrs)
- Friday: Edit tutorial (4-5 hrs)
- Saturday: Finalize tutorial, upload (3 hrs)
- Sunday: Monitor launch, respond to comments (1 hr)

**Batch Production Efficiency:**
- Film 12 Shorts once per month (one 4-hour session)
- Film 4 tutorial A-rolls in one weekend (shared setup)
- Use Canva templates for thumbnails (save 50% of time)

**Performance Tracking:**
- Weekly metrics: views, subs, watch time, CTR, AVD, traffic sources
- Optimization triggers (if CTR < 5%, test new thumbnails)
- Success benchmarks (month-by-month growth targets)

**Collaboration Opportunities (Month 3+):**
- Pet influencer collabs (10K-100K followers)
- Photography channel cross-promos
- Pet podcast guest appearances

**Contingency Plans:**
- If growth slower: Increase to 5x/week Shorts, run YouTube Ads, giveaways
- If growth faster: Hire editor, increase tutorial frequency, launch memberships

## Key Insights

### Why This Will Work:

1. **Proven Format:** Before/after transformations are inherently satisfying and shareable
2. **SEO Advantage:** "AI pet portraits" is growing search term with low competition
3. **Cross-Platform Synergy:** YouTube drives traffic to website, website drives YouTube subscribers
4. **Content Longevity:** Evergreen tutorials generate views for years (passive traffic)
5. **Monetization Diversity:** Ad revenue + direct sales + potential sponsorships

### Competitive Advantages:

1. **Specialized Niche:** Most AI art channels are generic. We're pet-specific.
2. **Production Quality:** Professional scripts, templates, and workflows ensure consistency
3. **Business Integration:** YouTube isn't just content — it's a sales funnel
4. **Batch Efficiency:** 12 Shorts in 4 hours = sustainable long-term production

### Success Factors:

1. **Consistency:** 3x/week Shorts + 1x/week tutorials (never miss a upload)
2. **Quality:** Production-ready templates ensure high baseline quality
