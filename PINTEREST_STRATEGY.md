## Phase 7: Analytics & Optimization (Ongoing)

### Track in Pinterest Analytics
- **Impressions:** How many times pins were seen
- **Saves:** How many times pins were saved (strong intent signal)
- **Clicks:** Outbound clicks to website
- **Top Pins:** Which images perform best
- **Top Boards:** Which boards drive traffic

### Success Metrics (Month 3)
- ✅ 10,000 monthly impressions
- ✅ 500 pin saves
- ✅ 200 outbound clicks to website
- ✅ 50 orders attributed to Pinterest (track with UTM: ?utm_source=pinterest)

### Optimization Loop (Weekly)
1. Identify top 10 performing pins
2. Create 5 variations of each (different text, colors, formats)
3. Repin top performers to new boards
4. Archive low performers (<10 impressions/week)
5. A/B test pin descriptions and keywords

### Collaborate with Pet Influencers
Reach out to top Pinterest pet accounts:
- Offer free custom portrait in exchange for pin
