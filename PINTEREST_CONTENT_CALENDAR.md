## Analytics Tracking (Weekly Check-Ins)

**Metrics to track:**
- **Impressions:** How many times pins were seen (goal: 1K/week → 5K/month)
- **Saves:** How many times pins were saved (goal: 50/week → 200/month)
- **Clicks:** Outbound clicks to website (goal: 20/week → 80/month)
- **Followers:** Board/account followers (goal: 50 by Day 30)
- **Top Pins:** Which images perform best?
- **Top Boards:** Which boards drive traffic?

**Weekly Optimization:**
1. Identify top 10 performing pins
2. Create 5 variations of each
3. Re-pin top performers to new boards
4. Archive pins with <5 impressions/week
